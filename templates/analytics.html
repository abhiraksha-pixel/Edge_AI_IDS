Analytics Interpretation

Important note about the simulated results

The analytics shown here are calculated from the traffic records stored by the Edge-AI gateway.

The current dataset contains earlier software simulation records and newer Wokwi ESP32 records. Therefore, the totals represent the records currently present in the log files.

The attack percentage describes the proportion of recorded simulated traffic classified as ATTACK. It is not a measurement of the attack rate on a real-world IoT network.

The telemetry-to-network-feature conversion used by the current project is a simulation adapter. It does not represent direct packet capture or deep inspection of real network traffic.
